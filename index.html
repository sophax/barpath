Step 2

Mark the lift

Scrub to just before the bar leaves the floor (for the jerk: just before the dip) and set the start. Then scrub to where you're standing and set the end. 2 to 4 seconds is ideal.

Start – · End –

Tips for good footage

Whole body and barbell in frame the whole time, camera on a tripod at about hip height, nobody walking through the shot. Side view is the most accurate. Slow motion (60 fps or more) improves the timing measurements.
